UK AI Research Symposium 2026


Call for Submissions – UK AI Research Symposium 2026
The UK AI Research Symposium (UKAIRS) will be held this year in Edinburgh at the John McIntyre Conference Centre on 24 - 25 November.
The Symposium will include themed parallel sessions, a presentation and Q&A on AI & Entrepreneurship, and a Community Panel chaired by Dawn Greenberg with panellists: Dr Christopher Burr, Dr Natasha McCarthy, Prof David Leslie, and Dr Nayha Sethi.

UKAIRS invites researchers, students, and practitioners to submit the following types of contributions: 

  • World Class Research Track (already published conference or journal paper): You will submit your peer-reviewed and published work (PDF). 

    • Emerging Research (2 page abstract): Research and early-stage research. You may propose a demo or a poster slot.

      Eligibility
      Students and researchers from institutions who have received or are eligible for UKRI AI investments (eg. all UK Universities) can submit under the World Class Research Track (already published conference or journal paper to present on) and/or Emerging Research Track (abstract for poster/demo). 

      For topics and submission details, go to: www.ukairs.ac.uk/i/CallforSubmissions  

       
      Important dates:

      ·         22 June 2026: Deadline for submitting papers, abstracts, posters, and demos

      ·         10 August 2026: Notification

      ·         13 September: Early Registration Deadline

       

      To submit, go to: https://www.ukairs.ac.uk/Submission. If you have any questions, please contact  APRIL@ed.ac.uk.

      Register here: www.efdelegates.ed.ac.uk/UKAIRS

      Entrepreneurship Workshop


      22 October, approx. 9:30am – 1:00pm, Room 3.18, 35 Berkeley Square

      Join SETsquared for a half-day workshop exploring how to turn research ideas into commercial opportunities. The session will cover the commercialisation process, intellectual property (IP), business planning and market research.

      You will learn about protecting and commercialising research, developing a value proposition, identifying potential customers and competitors, and evaluating market opportunities. Participants will also receive practical tools and resources, supporting materials, and access to advice from experienced mentors and legal specialists.

      The cost is £120 with limited places available. The cost will be charged to your RTSG.

      If you wish to attend, please sign up using the online form and let us know.

      Join the Data Ethics Club organising team


      The Data Ethics Club is looking for new organisers from all AI CDT cohorts, as Huw Day and Jessica Woodgate will be stepping down soon.

      The club meets monthly to discuss the ethical use of data science, using papers, blog posts, books, podcasts and videos to spark discussion. It’s a great opportunity for PhD students interested in ethics to get involved and help select reading materials and prepare discussion questions.

      Organisers share tasks including planning meetings, maintaining the website and GitHub repository, promoting sessions and writing up discussions. The commitment is around two hours per month, in addition to attending meetings. No special skills are needed, and tasks can be shared based on your interests.

      Interested in joining? Find out more about how the club is organised or email the team.
