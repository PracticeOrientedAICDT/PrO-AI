Protect and Exploit Your Research Results


Tuesday 8 December 2026, 1 - 2pm, Online (Microsoft Teams)

Join Abraham Mauleon Amieva, Research Commercialisation Manager at Bristol Innovations, for a session on protecting software intellectual property. He will explore different ways to protect software, including automatic legal rights, registered protections and confidentiality agreements.

The session will also introduce the Research Commercialisation team and explain how to engage with them, followed by a Q&A.

If you have topics you would like to see covered in a future How-To Session, or would like to contribute, please email cs-staff-enquiries@bristol.ac.uk.



Transforming Business and Investment into a Force for Good


27 January 2027, 6:30 - 7:45pm, Bill Brown Forum, Sloane Robinson Building

Join Erinch Sahan for a public talk hosted by Professor Martin Parker of the University of Bristol Business School, exploring how business and investment can be transformed into a force for good.

The talk is open to University staff, students, alumni and members of the public. Friends, family and colleagues are also welcome.

Bristol Doctoral College (BDC) Events and Opportunities


1. Finishing Your Research Degree

12 November 2026, 2:00–4:00pm,  Senate House (PGR Hub) / Online

Are you approaching the final stages of your research degree? Join the Bristol Doctoral College for this workshop to help you prepare for the milestones ahead and plan for a successful finish.

The session will cover strategies for staying motivated, overcoming writing barriers and completing your thesis, as well as the submission process and what to expect at your viva. It will also help you consider how to translate the skills and experience gained during your research into future career opportunities, while connecting with PGRs from across the University.

Book your tickets here for face to face session.

Book your tickets here for online session.

 

2. Beyond the Thesis: Deciding What’s Next

17 November 2026, 10:30–11:30am, Online via Teams

Unsure about your next career step after your PhD? Join the Bristol Doctoral College for an introductory session on career decision-making, particularly suited to full-time PhD students in their second or third year.

The session will explore how career decisions are made, help you develop criteria for evaluating different career options, and highlight sources of support and useful resources. It will also look at UK and Bristol PGR graduate destinations data, including where and with whom doctoral graduates find employment.

Book your tickets here.

 

3. Responding Effectively to Feedback on Your Writing

24 November 2026, 10:00am–12:00nn, Senate House (PGR Hub)

Getting feedback on your writing is an important part of developing as a researcher. Join the Centre for Academic Language and Development (CALD) for this interactive workshop, open to PGRs at any stage of their research degree.

Using examples of feedback you have received, the session will help you interpret comments, decide which suggestions to take forward and revise your writing effectively.

Please bring examples of feedback you have received on your writing to use during the session. Refreshments will be provided during a short break.

Book your tickets here.

4. Mature PGR Student Meet-up

25 November 2026, 2:30–3:30pm, Senate House (PGR Hub)

Are you a mature postgraduate researcher looking to connect with others on a similar journey? Join the first meet-up of the academic year organised by the Mature PGR Student Network. It’s an opportunity to meet fellow mature PGRs, share experiences and discuss the challenges of being a mature student. Tea, coffee and pastries will be provided.

Book your tickets here.

Call for Researchers: FUTURES Schools Research Fair


Application deadline: 5pm, Monday 16 November

Researchers are invited to take part in the FUTURES Schools Research Fair, an annual event giving hundreds of children from local primary schools the opportunity to meet researchers and explore hands-on activities and demonstrations.

The FUTURES team is looking for interactive stalls that will engage and inspire young visitors. Your idea does not need to be fully developed, as the team will work with you to refine it.
