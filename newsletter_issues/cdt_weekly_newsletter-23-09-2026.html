Find out more and apply now.

Viva Survivor

Wednesday 1 July, 10 am to 1 pm, online

The viva is almost the end of a research degree journey. A lot of work leads to this point, but often anxiety can mix with the sense of achievement at completing the thesis or dissertation. In this three-hour live webinar, you will learn:

  • realistic expectations for the viva

  • identify key practical steps to take before submission

  • explore practical strategies for preparation and the day of the viva.  

Find out more and book your place

Paid AI and Data Support Opportunity for University of Bristol PhD Students


Application deadline Monday 10 August

The Jean Golding Institute (JGI) are recruiting a new team of PhD students for the Ask-JGI helpdesk to work from October 2026 until the end of September 2027!

The JGI offers a consultancy service to researchers through its Ask-JGI helpdesk, which provides one day of free support to all staff and doctoral students at the University of Bristol for queries relating to data science, AI and software engineering. The helpdesk is run by PhD students and supported by the JGI’s own team of data scientists and research software engineers.

This is a paid opportunity at Research Support Assistant Level 2 for PhD students.

Visit the recruitment webpage for full details of the role, eligibility criteria and application process.

Read reflections from current Ask-JGI team members to hear first-hand what it is like to work on the helpdesk.

Around Bristol


As part of the celebration to mark the 150th year of the University of Bristol, artist Luke Jerram will be exhibiting his new work Pollenarium at University Square on Saturday 10 October. 
 
You can also hear Luke Jerram in-conversation with Nick Wray, curator of the Bristol Botanic Garden, for an inspiring insight into pollinators, creativity and the health of our planet. 
 
The celebration will also welcome renowned public historian, author, presenter and film-maker Professor David Olusoga (Hon LLD 2025) to the University’s Clifton Campus, for a rare in-person conversation with Dr Marie-Annick Gournet, Associate Pro Vice-Chancellor (Reparative and Civic Futures). 

These events are part of a free public programme of events across our Clifton campus and new Temple Quarter Enterprise Campus on Saturday 10 October – you can see the full programme of events here
