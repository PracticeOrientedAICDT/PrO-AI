AI Connect: Research Meets Industry


26 - 27 November 2026, Burg Frankenberg, Aachen, Germany

AI Connect: Research Meets Industry will bring together around 50 young AI researchers, company representatives and partners from across the AI ecosystem. Participants will explore real-world challenges presented by Carglass, Unchained Robotics, JUPITER AI Factory (JAIF) and Cognita Reply, and consider how AI research could lead to practical solutions.

The programme will include presentations and keynotes, interactive small-group workshops, group pitches and opportunities to meet researchers, companies and ecosystem partners.

The event will be held in English and participation is free of charge. Places are limited and participants will be selected from applications received.

Application deadline: 9 October 2026
Find out more about the company challenges and apply here.

For questions about the event or your application, please contact Marie Anastacio at anastacio@aim.rwth-aachen.de.

BDFI Guest Lecture: Inside the Making of Two EU AI Laws


Monday 5 October, 3:30 - 4:30pm, G.07, The Sheds, 65 Avon St, BS2 0PZ

BDFI is pleased to welcome Professor Ali Knott from Victoria University of Wellington for a guest lecture exploring the development of two EU laws regulating AI: the AI Act and the Digital Services Act (DSA).

Professor Knott will discuss his involvement with the Global Partnership on AI in developing policy around AI-generated content and research access to data held by large technology platforms. The talk will explore the challenges of making AI-generated content detectable and the mechanisms that allow researchers to access private platform data to study systemic risks.

The talk will be followed by refreshments and informal networking.

Please RSVP if you would like to attend.
