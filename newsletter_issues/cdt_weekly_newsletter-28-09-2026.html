Turing Opportunities


 

1. Turing Internship Network - Applications now open!

Looking to apply your research skills beyond academia?

 

The Turing Internship Network (TIN) is offering 11 paid internship opportunities with the Alan Turing Institute (6 positions) and the UK Ministry of Justice (5 positions).

 

These exciting 3 -12 month paid internships provide the chance to work on real-world challenges across the justice, sustainability and defence sectors, and gain new skills in a business setting.

 

Find out more and apply here

 

2. Turing Institute Funded AI Opportunity for Government Challenges

 

The Alan Turing Institute is inviting challenge proposals for a Data Study Group (DSG) taking place in March 2027 as part of the Open Source AI Fellowship Programme.

 

A DSG is a fully funded five-day research sprint that brings together multidisciplinary AI researchers to tackle real-world problems, generate insights, and produce openly licensed outputs.

 

The Institute is seeking data-rich challenges that support government priorities and could benefit from AI and data science approaches.

 

If you have a challenge that may be suitable, please review the opportunity details and submit a challenge proposal to datastudygroup@turing.ac.uk by 30 September 2026.

Find out more about the Data Study Group programme and submit a proposal.

 

This opportunity would be covered by the Open Source AI Fellowship grant, funded by Meta.

 



Research Capability Hub (RCH) Connect 2026


21 October 2026, 9:30am to 4:00pm| Swansea University and Online

Registration is now open for RCH Connect 2026, the first annual event hosted by the Research Capability Hub (RCH). Bringing together social science researchers, training providers and organisations from across the UK and beyond.

This event will explore the future of social science research training and capability, with interactive sessions, discussions, networking and opportunities to help shape the development of the Hub. It may be of particular interest to those looking to engage with social science research, broaden interdisciplinary perspectives, or develop collaborative projects across disciplines.

The event will be held at Swansea University, with an online option also available.
