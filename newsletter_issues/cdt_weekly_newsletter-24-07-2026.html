Internship opportunity (part-time) – XAI in Insurance Fraud


We are currently working on a collaboration with Allianz Insurance exploring explainable AI in Insurance Fraud investigation workflows
As part of this project we have an opening for a paid part-time intern (about 2 days/week during August and September) to work with us on a research project exploring how to make current explainable AI methods more user-friendly. We are using LLMs to generate explanatory text that fraud handlers a better understanding of a fraud alert model’s decision based on SHAP values and contextual information of the model. The intern’s job will be to extend existing python XAI libraries to include LLM generated explanations and experiment with different models and datasets. The aim is to find out what types of information will be the most effective and useful to generate explanations and how to best deliver this information within an LLM prompt.
 
What will you gain? You will receive insights and hands-on experience of an applied context of XAI methods, LLM prompting and human-computer interaction. You will benefit from mentoring from senior academics and industry partners’ data science team.
 
What will you bring? Skills in Python, explainable AI and/or LLM prompting.
 
If interested, please contact: Dr Vanessa Hanscke - vanessa.hanschke@bristol.ac.uk, Dr Zahraa Abdallah - zahraa.abdallah@bristol.ac.uk or Professor Kenton O’Hara – kenton.ohara@bristol.ac.uk
